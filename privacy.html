Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes how CoupleGuard processes information when used as a Reddit community moderation assistant.

2. Information processed

Depending on the available Reddit event and API responses, CoupleGuard may process post identifiers, titles, authors, subreddit names, post URLs, NSFW and spam indicators, available image URLs and dimensions, and account age and karma information.

3. AI image analysis

For supported image posts, CoupleGuard may retrieve images and send image content to Google's Gemini API for analysis. The analysis is intended to estimate apparent safety and whether two or more people are visible. The result may include a recommendation, an explanation, and the number of images analyzed.

Do not submit confidential information with the expectation that CoupleGuard will keep it confidential. Processing by Google is subject to Google's applicable API terms and privacy documentation.

4. Moderation reports and logs

CoupleGuard generates advisory moderation reports. Its current configuration is report-only and does not automatically approve or remove posts. Report information may be written to application logs for debugging and moderation evaluation. Access, availability, and retention of those logs depend on the hosting platform and configured services.

5. API credentials

The Gemini API credential is configured as an application secret rather than intended for public display. Never publish API keys in this repository, source files, screenshots, or public reports.

6. Sharing and service providers

Image content submitted for AI analysis is shared with Google's Gemini API. Reddit processes information under its own policies. CoupleGuard's developer does not control the independent data practices of these providers.

7. Security and retention

Reasonable care should be taken to protect application credentials and access to reports. No online service can guarantee absolute security. This policy does not promise a specific deletion period for platform or third-party logs.

8. Children's privacy

CoupleGuard is a moderation tool, not a service intended for children to use directly. Moderators should follow Reddit's rules and applicable laws when handling content involving minors.

9. Policy changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the CoupleGuard developer profile on Reddit.
