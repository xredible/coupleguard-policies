Terms and Conditions

Effective date: October 21, 2026

1. About CoupleGuard

CoupleGuard is a Reddit community moderation assistant designed to help moderators review submissions using post metadata, account information available to the app, and automated image analysis.

2. Advisory decisions

CoupleGuard may generate pass, review, or flag recommendations. These recommendations are automated assessments and may be inaccurate, incomplete, or inconsistent with a community's rules. A pass is not a guarantee that content complies with all rules, and a flag is not proof of a violation.

The current configuration is report-only. CoupleGuard does not automatically approve or remove posts. Reddit, subreddit moderators, and other Reddit systems may take separate actions.

3. Image analysis

Images may be analyzed by an external AI service to assess apparent safety and whether two or more people are visible. These assessments cannot establish whether people are a couple, verify identity, or determine consent or relationship status. AI results should be reviewed by a human moderator.

4. Acceptable use

Users and moderators must follow Reddit's applicable terms, policies, and community rules. Do not rely on CoupleGuard as the sole basis for consequential moderation decisions.

5. Availability and accuracy

The service is provided on an as-available basis. Image analysis, network requests, and third-party services may fail or return incorrect results. When required information is missing or uncertain, the configured rules may recommend human review.

6. Third-party services

CoupleGuard relies on Reddit and may use Google's Gemini API for image analysis. Those services are subject to their own terms and policies.

7. Changes and contact

These terms may be updated as CoupleGuard changes. For questions, contact the developer through the CoupleGuard developer profile on Reddit.
